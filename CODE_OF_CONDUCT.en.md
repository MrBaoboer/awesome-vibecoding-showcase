# Contributor Covenant 3.0 Code of Conduct

Chinese version: [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make our community welcoming, safe, and equitable for all.

We respect the dignity, rights, and contributions of every individual, regardless of race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socio-economic position, level of education, or other status. Everyone who participates in good faith and follows this Code has the same right to participate.

## Encouraged Behaviors

We understand that culture, background, and native language can affect how words and actions are understood. We therefore agree to:

1. Respect the community's purpose, activities, and ways of working.
2. Engage kindly and honestly with others.
3. Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
4. Take responsibility for our actions and contributions.
5. Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
6. Work to repair harm when it occurs.
7. Promote and sustain the well-being of the community in other ways.

## Prohibited Behaviors

Instances, threats, or promotion of the following behaviors violate this Code:

1. **Harassment:** Violating expressed boundaries or continuing unnecessary personal attention after a clear request to stop.
2. **Character attacks:** Directing insulting, demeaning, or pejorative comments at an individual or group.
3. **Stereotyping or discrimination:** Characterizing someone's personality or behavior on the basis of immutable identities or traits.
4. **Sexualization:** Behaving in a way that is inappropriately intimate for the community's context or purpose.
5. **Violating confidentiality:** Sharing or acting on personal or private information without permission.
6. **Endangerment:** Causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or other harm toward a person or group.
7. Other behavior that threatens the well-being of the community.

The following are also prohibited: impersonating others or evading enforcement; failing to credit contributed content; sharing marketing or commercial material outside community norms; and irresponsibly presenting content that includes, links to, or describes the behaviors above.

## Reporting an Issue

Report a possible violation privately through the repository's GitHub page:

1. Open **Security**, then **Advisories**, then **Report a vulnerability**.
2. Prefix the title with **[Code of Conduct]**.
3. Include the time, location, factual description, necessary evidence, immediate safety concern, and requested resolution.
4. Remove secrets, unrelated personal data, and private third-party material.

If private reporting is unavailable, open a public issue titled **[Private conduct report request]**. The body must only request a private channel; do not identify people, describe the incident, or attach evidence. The maintainer will create a private security advisory and invite the reporter to continue there.

If the report concerns the lead maintainer, do not send sensitive details through a channel controlled by that maintainer. For conduct on GitHub, use GitHub's **Report abuse** feature.

Community moderators will:

- acknowledge a report within 3 calendar days and provide an initial status or next step within 14 calendar days;
- share information only with people needed for a fair response;
- give the people involved a reasonable opportunity to provide relevant facts unless doing so creates a safety risk;
- record and carry out decisions privately, with only necessary public communication;
- disclose conflicts of interest and recuse in favor of an unrelated moderator.

Decisions are evidence-based and consider impact, context, pattern, power imbalance, safety, accountability, and willingness to repair harm. Knowingly false reports, retaliation, or public exposure of the people involved may themselves violate this Code. An unsubstantiated report is not, by itself, a false report.

## Enforcement

Moderators may apply the following measures according to impact and severity, and may skip lower levels:

| Level | When it applies | Consequence and repair |
|---|---|---|
| Warning | A single incident or series of incidents violates this Code | Private written warning; may require an apology, acknowledgment of responsibility, or clarification of expectations |
| Temporarily limited activities | A violation recurs after a warning, or a more serious violation occurs for the first time | Time-limited restrictions on specific channels or interactions, with return conditions |
| Temporary suspension | Violations continue despite prior action, or a single serious violation occurs | Suspension from community access with written return conditions |
| Permanent ban | Other measures cannot resolve repeated violations, or continued participation would endanger the community | Removal of access to community spaces, tools, and communication channels |

Project popularity, sponsorship, and contributor status do not alter enforcement.

## Scope

This Code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, commits, and project-operated communication channels. It also applies when someone officially represents the community in public.
