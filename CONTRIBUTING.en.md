# Contributing

[Chinese version](CONTRIBUTING.md)

## Submit a project

[Open the project submission form](https://github.com/MrBaoboer/Awesome-VibeCoding-Showcase/issues/new?template=project-submission.yml) and submit one project per issue. See the [admission criteria](docs/CRITERIA.en.md) for eligibility and [governance](GOVERNANCE.en.md) for review targets and appeals. The maintainer handles classification, catalog data, and showcase updates.

Use public development notes, issues, pull requests, commits, or redacted process records as AI development evidence. Never submit secrets, access tokens, private conversations, or personal data.

## Correct existing content

[Open the general issue form](https://github.com/MrBaoboer/Awesome-VibeCoding-Showcase/issues/new?template=general-issue.yml) to report broken links, factual errors, project updates, classification problems, or removal concerns. Include a public source. Report vulnerabilities privately under the [security policy](SECURITY.en.md).

## Open a pull request

Pull requests are for copy, translation, configuration, or reviewed catalog changes. A new listing must link to a project-submission issue labeled `status: approved`; an update to an existing listing may link to its review issue or a general issue. Keep the change focused and explain why it is needed. Do not edit generated showcase regions in the READMEs.

All contributions must follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.en.md). By contributing, you confirm that you may publish the material and agree to its distribution under the repository's applicable licenses.
