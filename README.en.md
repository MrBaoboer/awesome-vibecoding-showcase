## Submit a project

Before submitting, confirm that the project meets every requirement:

- a public product or demo is available;
- a public repository contains the product's main implementation;
- AI implemented most core functionality through natural-language collaboration;
- the core flow works, with concrete evidence of completeness, utility, or innovation;
- the product, source, description, and development evidence are authentic and consistent.

To submit:

1. Search the showcase and existing issues to avoid duplicates.
2. [Open the project submission form](https://github.com/MrBaoboer/Awesome-VibeCoding-Showcase/issues/new?template=project-submission.yml) and provide the problem, features and value, technology stack, AI development workflow, live URL, and source URL.

Submit one project per issue. You do not need to choose a category or open a pull request; the maintainer will review the issue, classify the project, and prepare the listing.

See the [admission criteria](docs/CRITERIA.en.md) and [project categories](docs/CATEGORIES.en.md) for details.
