## What this repository is

`Awesome-VibeCoding-Showcase` is a GitHub-native, Chinese-first showcase with key content available in English. It focuses on what people have built. AI coding tools, IDEs, editors, plugins, and models are out of scope.

It serves:

- developers who want to try credible Vibe Coding products and inspect their source and build evidence;
- makers who want to present a finished product and explain how AI contributed to its implementation;
- teams and community maintainers studying product quality, technical choices, and human–AI workflows.

Why Star it: every listing includes a live experience, public source, evidence of substantial AI involvement, a consistent value summary, and a verification date. Broken or misleading entries enter a documented review and removal process.

## Admission criteria

A project must pass all five criteria:

- **C1 — Public experience:** a publicly accessible product, demo, or live experience exists;
- **C2 — Public source:** a public repository contains substantive source matching the product;
- **C3 — Substantial AI implementation:** AI generated most core functionality through iterative dialogue, or natural language drove the main implementation;
- **C4 — Outcome quality:** the product demonstrates verifiable completeness, utility, or innovation;
- **C5 — Verifiable truth:** the product, source, author claims, and development evidence are mutually consistent.

Every listing must also explain the problem (D1), features and practical value (D2), main technology stack (D3), the AI tools/depth/workflow (D4), and both live and source links (D5). See the [criteria and evidence guide](docs/CRITERIA.en.md).

Documentation-only use, comment generation, extra tests, minor bug fixes, or asset generation does not satisfy C3. There are no categories for tools, IDEs, plugins, or models.

## Submit a project

1. Read the [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.en.md) and self-check C1–C5.
2. Open **Issues → New issue** and choose **项目提交 / Project submission**.
3. Submit one project per issue, with public and redacted evidence of the AI development process.
4. After the issue receives `status: approved`, follow the pull request template to update structured data and bilingual output.

The target is an initial response within 7 days and a complete decision within 14 days after all required evidence is available. Passing automation does not mean the project has been approved.

## Review and maintenance

- New submissions follow the [human review checklist](docs/REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md) for C1–C5 and D1–D5.
- Links are checked automatically every week; the maintainer manually rechecks at least 10 listings each month.
- Ordinary link failures are confirmed twice before notification and receive a 14-day repair window.
- Closed source, false evidence, or security risk triggers removal review.
- Listing disputes are public by default; conduct and security reports use private channels.

See [governance](GOVERNANCE.md) for states, deadlines, recusal, and appeals.
